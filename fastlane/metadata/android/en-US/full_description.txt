RadioThing is an internet radio player styled like an instrument panel from a black lab bench: a dot-matrix typeface, hairline grids, and a CRT-style equalizer that dances to the stream's real audio spectrum.

It tunes into the community-run radio-browser.info directory — tens of thousands of live stations worldwide.

FEATURES

• Browse and search the global station directory
• Filter by country, genre, language, bitrate and codec
• Live FFT-driven dot equalizer in Now Playing
• Favorites with undo and an automatic listening log
• Playlists ("trays") — play a whole tray as a queue
• Sleep timer (5 / 15 / 30 / 60 min) with gentle fade-out
• Mini player with swipe-up expand and queue navigation
• Pull-to-refresh, endless pagination, offline-friendly caching

PRIVACY

• No ads, no trackers, no analytics
• Only talks to radio-browser.info and the stations you play
• No accounts, no permissions beyond network and playback

Station data courtesy of the open radio-browser.info community. App code is GPL-3.0; the interface uses the OFL-licensed Doto typeface.
